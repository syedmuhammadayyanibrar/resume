ENGINEERING MANIFESTO

How I think about AI engineering

A lot of modern AI development has fallen into the trap of superficial wrapper architecture—gluing prompt templates together and hoping non-deterministic LLMs figure out the rest. In production, enterprise legal workflows, and mission-critical accessibility, that breaks down immediately.

My philosophy is that autonomous AI systems must have deterministic boundaries: multi-agent federations with dynamic mesh arbitration, cryptographic evidence-grounded compliance audits, strict pre-execution invariants, and tactile edge ergonomics.

Whether architecting 6-society contract negotiation federations, autonomous EU AI Act compliance auditors, or offline multimodal assistants for blind shopkeepers, I prioritize formal verification, observability, test coverage, and systems that execute reliably under real-world constraints.
