ENGINEERING MANIFESTO

How I think about AI engineering

A lot of modern AI development has fallen into the trap of superficial wrapper architecture—gluing prompt templates together and hoping non-deterministic LLMs figure out the rest. In production and real-world environments, that breaks down immediately.

My philosophy is that autonomous AI systems must have deterministic boundaries: clear convergence invariants, game-theoretic negotiation rules, structured execution graphs, and self-evaluating gates before any irreversible action is taken.

Whether building on-device multimodal assistants that run offline for blind shopkeepers or coordinating distributed multi-agent swarms, I prioritize observability, tactile ergonomics, test coverage, and architectures that perform reliably when conditions are unconstrained.
